# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the Valider project respectful, constructive,
and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what is best for the project.
- Keep examples and testing within domains you are authorized to assess.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, doxxing, unwanted attention, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and
may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository maintainers. They will review reports fairly and confidentially
to the extent practical.
